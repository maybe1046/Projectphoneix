# Recruit additional accounts and helpers

Type: task
Status: open
Blocked by: none

## Question

Coverage scales with accounts and people far better than it scales with one person's hours.
A single account cannot be in two zones at once, cannot see both factions, cannot hold
every profession, and cannot survive a ban.

Establish what is actually available: how many accounts, what character levels and
factions, which professions, and who among the user's contacts still plays and would run a
capture build or the ticket-10 export addon before the fifth.

Already in motion — the user is asking around. Recorded as a ticket because the answer
gates how ambitious the ticket-06 coverage checklist can be, and because a helper recruited
on day four is worth a fraction of one recruited on day one.
