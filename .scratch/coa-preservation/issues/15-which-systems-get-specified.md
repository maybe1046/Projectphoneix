# Which CoA systems get specified, and in what order?

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 08

## Question

After the servers are gone, the archive will be larger than the appetite to document it.
Decide what gets specified first, and be honest about what may never get specified at all.

Inputs: what the captures actually covered; what turned out to be recoverable from client
files alone; and which systems the user personally cares about — a question asked during
charting and not yet answered, which matters precisely because it decides what to cut.

Ordering should follow dependency, not enthusiasm: auth and character login gate
everything, so they come first regardless of how much less interesting they are than the
draft or the skill cards.
