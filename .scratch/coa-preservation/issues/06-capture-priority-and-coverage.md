# What gets captured, in what order, and how do we know we got it?

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 02, 03, 04, 05

## Question

With ~50 hours, a known opcode table, a chosen toolchain, and knowledge of what the scrape
and the wider community already provide — decide the capture plan.

What is the priority order across: the login and character-select sequence; world data the
server pushes and the client never persists (creature, gameobject, quest, item); each
custom system exercised deliberately (draft, hand of fate, skill cards, mystic enchants,
wildcard, transmog, rulesets, player polls); social and economic systems (auction house,
guild, mail); and instanced content.

Produce a concrete coverage checklist — an in-game shot list, keyed to opcode ids from
ticket 05 — so a session can be executed without deciding anything mid-capture, and so
coverage can be measured rather than felt. Sessions that need a decision made halfway
through are sessions that waste the budget.

Also settle: which activities go on the secondary account, and what happens to the plan if
the main account is banned on day one.
